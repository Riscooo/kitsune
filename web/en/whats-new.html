What's new in Kitsune

What each version brings, from the newest to the first. Kitsune tells you by itself when a new one is out: once a day it checks this site's download list, without cookies or identifiers.

0.8 · coming next

With Gecko 158, the Firefox 158 engine and its security fixes.

A lot open, never lost.

  • Spaces: groups of tabs, each with its name, its color and, if you like, its own user. Switch with a click or with Ctrl Alt 1…9.
  • Tabs on the side: on top, in a column on one side or just the icons, from Appearance.
  • Split view: up to four sites at once (three in columns, four in a grid). Ctrl Alt D splits the tab or adds another site; with several selected, it puts them together.
  • Glance: Alt and click a link to open it on top, without a new tab.
  • Palette: Ctrl K finds tabs from every space, bookmarks, history and actions, each with its shortcut.
  • Compact mode: just the site; the toolbars come back when the mouse reaches the edge. Ctrl Alt C.
  • Now playing: when music or a video plays in another tab, its title, its cover and the buttons, at the bottom of the sidebar. One click takes you there, even from another space.
  • Bring your stuff: bookmarks, history and tabs from Zen (with its spaces), Firefox, LibreWolf, Floorp or Waterfox; from Chrome, Edge or Brave, with the import wizard.

And also.

  • Inks: six styles for all of Kitsune, with their colors in Light and in Dark and their shape (corners and lettering). And you can make your own. Plus a compact density.
  • Saver mode you can see: the memory Kitsune uses in the toolbar, sleeping tabs dimmed and «Never sleep» for the sites you want awake. With 20 sites, half the memory it uses without it, and less than Chrome.
  • Welcome: the first time, your home page lets you pick the theme, the ink and where the tabs go.
  • Kitsune copy: your bookmarks, your spaces with their tabs and, if you like, your history and passwords, in a file encrypted with your password to take them to another computer. No server: you carry the file.
  • Privacy you can see: your home page tells you how many trackers it stopped this week.
  • Properly in English: the home page, its help and everything new. A new profile follows the system language; the ones you already had stay as they were.
