What's new

What's new in Kitsune

What each version brings, from the newest to the first. Kitsune tells you by itself when a new one is out: when it opens and every quarter of an hour it checks this site's download list, without cookies or identifiers.

0.8 beta 5 · October 23, 2026

Faster: built like Firefox. Kitsune was built without the optimizations Mozilla builds Firefox with. Now it has the same ones: PGO with Mozilla's official profile for each version and system, LTO across C++ and Rust, the startup order and SIMD in Rust. In Speedometer 3.1 it goes from 13.3 to 17.8, and in JetStream 3.0 from 128.7 to 140.8 (same PC, same Gecko).

  • Spotify plays in Kitsune Zero's music panel: the panel has its own «Enable DRM» bar.
  • The new-version notice shows up right away: 10 seconds after opening Kitsune and every quarter of an hour.
  • Windows updates itself, like Linux: the new version installs when you close Kitsune.
  • In Zero, the glass follows the page's scroll, the pomodoro tells you when a strict break ends, and the sound panel stops repainting while closed.

Also. Gecko 158.0.1, with its security fixes.

0.8 beta 4 · October 22, 2026

Kitsune Zero, light and dark. «Style» is now Light or Dark, with the same frame in both. Light has see-through glass pieces and pages in light; Dark has smoked glass and pages in dark, and pages that only come in light are darkened (photos keep their colours).

  • Spotify plays in Strict: DRM stays off until a site asks for it; then «Enable DRM» appears and one click makes it play (Netflix, Apple Music, Deezer and Tidal too). In Kitsune and Kitsune Zero.
  • Privacy, Strict or Comfortable, now also in Kitsune Zero's settings.
  • Music at its width: Zero's music panel takes the width each service needs; Spotify is no longer cut off.
  • No line under the search bar in Zero when you have more than one space.
  • Kitsune, a first-class dark mode: Pure black as a third theme, the «Darken light pages» option and menus one step above.

Also. Gecko 158.0.1, with its security fixes.

0.8 beta 3 · October 16, 2026

Kitsune Zero, a separate browser. The same engine as Kitsune, with its own icon and its own installation: you can have both. All its windows are Zero, and the first time you open it, it brings a copy of your Kitsune profile (sign-ins, tabs and passwords). Kitsune itself no longer has the Zero mode.

  • Two styles, with everything the same: Nacre (light glass with the rim of a soap bubble) or Sober (smoked glass, calmer). You choose in its settings.
  • Grab a page: the little bar under the page takes it to one side (two sites at once) or to its own window. And a tab from the column, dropped on the page, goes next to it.
  • Work and Chill with two buttons (a briefcase and a moon), music and sound together in one pill (with each tab's volume), your shortcuts and the settings at the end of the column.
  • Backgrounds: Space, Mist or your own photo, with its opacity; and your profile above the search box of a new tab, with its email and accounts in its card.
  • Each system its own way: window corners like those of your system and, on a Mac, the green button goes full screen with Zero still there.

0.8 beta 2 · October 15, 2026

Kitsune Zero. A new way to use Kitsune, without bars: the page on a card over a sheet of glass, and everything else in glass pieces that show up when needed. Turn it on and off with Ctrl Alt 0, per window.

  • The island: on top, the address and what is going on (loading, downloads, what is playing, camera and microphone in use). With nothing open, a search box in the middle.
  • The left column: your profiles stacked, the Work/Chill switch, your tabs and your shortcuts.
  • Profiles with their own sessions: each profile can have its own Google, cookies and sign-ins.
  • Work and Chill: the pomodoro, quiet mode (silenced notifications and blocked sites while you work) and background sounds, each profile with its own mode.
  • Music like in Opera: Spotify, YouTube Music, Apple Music, SoundCloud, Deezer or Tidal in their own panel, plus a floating mini player; the music keeps playing when you close it.
  • Real glass: the edge like a soap bubble, with what lies behind bent along it like real glass: with Apple's glass on macOS 26, with your desktop's on Linux (niri, Hyprland, KDE or GNOME) and, on Windows, over a picture of the page.

And also. Links from other apps open with the sessions of the tab you are on, and on Linux with Wayland a transparent window no longer stays black after switching apps.

0.8 beta 1 · October 14, 2026

With Gecko 158, the Firefox 158 engine and its security fixes.

A lot open, never lost.

  • Spaces: groups of tabs, each with its name, its color, its emoji and, if you like, its own user. Switch with a click or with Ctrl Alt 1…9.
  • Tabs on the side: on top, in a column on one side or just the icons, from Appearance.
  • Split view: up to four sites at once (three in columns, four in a grid). Ctrl Alt D splits the tab or adds another site; with several selected, it puts them together.
  • Glance: Alt and click a link to open it on top, without a new tab. If you like it, it stays as a tab without reloading.
  • Palette: Ctrl K finds tabs from every space, bookmarks, history and actions, each with its shortcut.
  • Compact mode: just the site; the toolbars come back when the mouse reaches the edge. Ctrl Alt C.
  • Now playing: when music or a video plays in another tab, its title, its cover and the buttons, at the bottom of the sidebar. One click takes you there, even from another space.
  • Bring your stuff: bookmarks, history and tabs from Zen (with its spaces), Firefox, LibreWolf, Floorp or Waterfox; from Chrome, Edge or Brave, with the import wizard.

And also.

  • Inks: six styles for all of Kitsune, with their colors in Light and in Dark and their shape (corners and lettering). And you can make your own. Plus a compact density.
  • Saver mode you can see: the memory Kitsune uses in the toolbar, sleeping tabs dimmed and «Never sleep» for the sites you want awake. With 20 sites, half the memory it uses without it, and less than Chrome.
  • Tweaks: floating web, centered address, new tabs next to the current one, Ctrl+Tab by recent use and more, one click away from the menu.
  • Your own keyboard shortcuts: Kitsune's and the most used ones, from the menu. It tells you if a combination already belongs to something else.
  • Welcome: the first time, your home page lets you pick the theme, the ink and where the tabs go.
  • Kitsune copy: your bookmarks, your spaces with their tabs and, if you like, your history and passwords, in a file encrypted with your password to take them to another computer. No server: you carry the file.
  • Privacy you can see: your home page tells you how many trackers it stopped this week.
  • Properly in English: the home page, its help and everything new. A new profile follows the system language; the ones you already had stay as they were.
